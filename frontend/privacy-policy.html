Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026  •  Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bethel Trading Technologies ("Bethel", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you visit our website, communicate with us, use our services, or authorize an integration with a third-party platform such as LinkedIn.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with Bethel, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, telephone number, account or onboarding information, support messages and other information you submit to us. We may also receive technical and usage information such as IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, referring pages, security logs and website interaction data.

2. LinkedIn and other authorized integrations

If an authorized user connects Bethel to LinkedIn, we may receive information made available through LinkedIn's APIs and the permissions granted during authorization. This may include identifiers and information needed to identify an authorized LinkedIn member or organization/Page, determine Page administration rights, publish or manage Page content, and measure or manage engagement where the approved API permissions allow it.

Bethel accesses LinkedIn data only through authorized permissions and for the purpose of providing the requested integration. We do not sell LinkedIn member or Page data. Access is subject to LinkedIn's platform terms, the permissions approved for our application, and the authorization provided by the relevant user or Page administrator.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, secure and improve Bethel websites, applications and services.
  • Authenticate users and maintain authorized integrations.
  • Publish, manage or monitor company content when an authorized Page administrator enables those functions.
  • Respond to inquiries, support requests and partnership communications.
  • Detect abuse, investigate security events, maintain records and comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Understand service performance and improve reliability, usability and user experience.

4. How we share information

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, security, communications, analytics, identity verification or other infrastructure needed to operate Bethel. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights or security, or in connection with a legitimate corporate transaction. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing services, maintaining security and audit records, resolving disputes and meeting legal or regulatory obligations. Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and the reason it is held.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw an authorization granted to a connected platform through that platform's account settings where available.

For requests relating to Bethel-held information or deletion of data associated with an authorized integration, contact us at info@betheltradingtechnologies.com. We may need to verify your identity or authority before completing a request.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website or service providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences and measurement. Browser controls may allow you to limit some of these technologies, although doing so may affect functionality.

9. International processing

Our technology and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Where required, we take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards for such processing.

10. Children's privacy

Bethel's services are not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

11. Third-party services

Our website and applications may link to or integrate with third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies and terms. This Policy does not control how those third parties independently process information.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, legal obligations or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to info@betheltradingtechnologies.com.
